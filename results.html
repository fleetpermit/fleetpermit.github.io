How to read these results

Pass means the observed outcome matched the expected one. Unsupported upstream means the scenario asks for something the pinned upstream release cannot enforce yet; the result is still recorded. For example, argument-level matching such as refusing replicas=1000 is not supported by kube-agentic-networking v0.2.0.

Scenario IDs are stable. The policy playground links to the scenario that exercises each rule, for example S3 for a wrong identity and S5 for expiry.

Reproduce the numbers with make demo-up, make test, make test-e2e, make benchmark and make conformance, then make results. The simulated scale rows come from envtest and a simulated OCM work agent. They measure the controller, not managed clusters.
